## Conclusion

Switching from SP1024 to the available SP8192 tokenizer/data gives a large quality gain at seq1024, but the default root-baseline int8+zlib artifact is 3,376,008 bytes over the 16MB limit. Treat this as a quality reference unless paired with stronger compression.

Note: PLAN.md listed a CaseOps SP8192 dataset/tokenizer, but those files were not present in the container. This run used the available regular SP8192 dataset and tokenizer.
